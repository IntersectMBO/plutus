### Added

- Added a simple ByteString library, with a focus on the basic operations necessary for formalizing the semantics of Cardano-specific builtins. The library also contains proofs for the correctness of these operations. It is not intended to be efficient as the focus is on the ease of writing specifications and proofs.
- Added formalizations of the "programmatic" (non-crypto, non-serialization) Cardano builtins in Agda, together with the relevant documentation extracted from the PDF spec.
