### Added

- Added `Value∞`, a mathematical model of values with no bounds on key lengths or quantities, as the foundation of the `Value` builtin type, with its operations and their properties: values are determined by their quantities, form a commutative group and a module over the integers, and are equivalent to their nested representation.
- Added a formalization of the `Value` builtin type and the semantics of its builtin functions (`insertCoin`, `lookupCoin`, `unionValue`, `valueContains`, `scaleValue`, `valueData`, `unValueData`, `policies`, `assetCount`, `keepPolicies` and `dropPolicies`), with proofs of their main properties.
- Added a formalization of the `Data` builtin type over the pure ByteString library, with decidable equality and the semantics of the `Data` builtin functions other than `serialiseData`.

### Changed

- Generalized the lexicographic order on ByteStrings and redefined it using the standard library, in the new `Builtin.ByteString.Order` module.
