### Added

- Added `Builtin.CByteString`, which formalises Cardano ByteStrings: ByteStrings of at most 65536 bytes. Also added wrappers for the ByteString builtins that require their arguments to be within this bound.

### Changed

- Made the bound proofs in `CInteger` irrelevant.

### Fixed

- `writeBits` in the Agda CEK machine now fails on inputs longer than 4096 bytes, as the Plutus Core evaluator does.
