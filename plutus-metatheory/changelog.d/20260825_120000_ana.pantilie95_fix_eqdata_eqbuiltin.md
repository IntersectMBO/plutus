### Fixed

- The compiled decidable equality for `Array` constants considered any two arrays
  equal, so the certifier accepted traces claiming unequal array constants equal:
  MAlonzo inlined the constantly-`true` Agda body of the equality primitive at its
  use site instead of the `(==)` from its `COMPILE GHC` pragma. Arrays are now
  compared structurally, by converting them to lists (via the new `HSarrayToList`
  postulate, with a postulated — and true — injectivity fact used only in proofs)
  and reusing the decision procedure for the element type. The `HasEq` FFI
  machinery is removed.

- `eqDATA` now computes correctly at the Agda level: it is defined via a new
  structural decision procedure (`eqDATA?`) instead of comparing bytestring
  leaves with a constantly-`true` function and comparing lists of different
  lengths via truncating `zipWith`. Its runtime behavior is unchanged: it
  still compiles to Haskell's `(==)`.

### Changed

- Decidable equality of the postulated builtin types (`ByteString`, the
  BLS12-381 element types and `Value`) is now obtained via the new
  `decEqFromBool` combinator in `Utils`, replacing the `builtinEq`/`HsEq`
  machinery and the unsound `magicNeg` postulate. The constantly-`true`
  Bool-valued FFI primitives are now private to their defining modules, so
  they cannot be misused in Agda-level code.

- The builtin denotations `equals`, `BLS12-381-G1-equal` and
  `BLS12-381-G2-equal` are no longer postulates: they are defined via the
  decision procedures, so they now reduce at the Agda level while still
  compiling to Haskell's `(==)`.
