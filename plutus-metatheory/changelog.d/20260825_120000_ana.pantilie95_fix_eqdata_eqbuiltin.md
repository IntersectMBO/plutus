### Fixed

- Fixed the runtime decidable equality of the postulated builtin types
  (`ByteString`, the BLS12-381 element types, `Value`, `Array`, and `Data`
  via its bytestring leaves), which the certifier uses to compare constants:
  the previous `primTrustMe`-based procedures compiled to a constant `yes`,
  making the certifier accept unequal constants. Equality of postulated types
  now goes through `decEqFromBool`, which defers to Haskell's `(==)` at
  runtime without relying on unsound postulates, replacing the removed
  `builtinEq`/`magicNeg` machinery. The underlying Bool-valued FFI primitives
  are private to their defining modules so they cannot be misused in
  Agda-level computation, and the now-unused `HsEq` layer was removed.
  The builtin denotations `equals`, `BLS12-381-G1-equal` and
  `BLS12-381-G2-equal` are no longer postulates: they are defined via the
  decision procedures (like `eqDATA`), so they now reduce correctly at the
  Agda level while still compiling to Haskell's `(==)`.
