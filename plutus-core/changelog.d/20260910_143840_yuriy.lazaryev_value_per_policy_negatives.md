### Changed

- `Value` now caches the number of negative amounts per currency, not only in total, which makes `keepPolicies` and `dropPolicies` logarithmic in the `Value` rather than linear in its size. The memory models of `insertCoin`, `unionValue`, `scaleValue` and `unValueData` account for the extra field.
- `keepPolicies` and `dropPolicies` fail on a `Value` with more than 8191 policies (`policyFilterMaxSize`); beyond that bound their cost models would undercharge.
