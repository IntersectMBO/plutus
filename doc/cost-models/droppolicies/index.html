DropPolicies Cost Model Visualization

Interactive 3D visualization of benchmark data and the fitted cost model for the DropPolicies builtin. The x-axis is the length of the policy list, the y-axis the depth of the Value's outer map, and the z-axis execution time. Every benchmark point appears twice at the same place on the floor: as a blue dot for what it measured, and as a red cross for what the model charges for it. Where a cross sits above its dot the model overcharges that input, and the fit is built to leave almost every dot below its cross.

The model is proportional to the product of the two: the builtin does one descent of the outer map per element of the list, and Map.updateLookupWithKey rebuilds the whole search path whether or not it finds the key, so the number of elements that name a policy the Value actually has does not enter. The depth is the measure the builtin's denotation uses, and it is 1 for the empty Value rather than 0: a product model would otherwise charge nothing but its intercept for a policy list of any length.
